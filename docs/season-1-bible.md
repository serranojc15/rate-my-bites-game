# Season 1 Bible

The permanent, machine-validated Season 1 continuity record lives in
[`season1Bible.js`](../season1Bible.js).

Season 1 is set in Huntsville and North Alabama. It records the timeline, recurring
restaurants, The Party roster, guest roster, relationships, established facts, running
jokes, important past events, mystery outcomes, unresolved hooks, future episode
ideas, and continuity constraints.

The official timeline is:

1. Episode 1 — Olivia’s promotion dinner at Casa Luna.
2. Episode 2 — June’s Willow Lake homecoming at Maple & Main.
3. Episode 3 — Emma, Ellis, and Grace at The Copper Table.

Continuity can reward a returning player, but every episode must make sense alone.
Prior events never contain required puzzle evidence. Living variations never change
the attending Party, restaurant truth, solution, ending, or continuity consequences.

The Season Bible is an authoring source, not an unrestricted runtime generator. Future
ideas remain non-playable until approved and implemented in a separately scoped
episode.
