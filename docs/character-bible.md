# Character Bible

The permanent, machine-validated Character Bible lives in
[`characterBible.js`](../characterBible.js). `worldBible.js` supplies fixed identity,
portrait, and appearance records; `characterBible.js` expands them into the complete
series profile used by The Party and episode production.

Every recurring profile defines:

- full canonical name as currently established
- fixed portrait
- personality and short personality summary
- favorite meals and drinks
- food preferences and dislikes
- humor and conversation style
- relationships and relationship to The Party
- running jokes and recurring habits
- character goals and story hooks
- continuity notes and episode appearances
- future growth opportunities
- a memorable quote and Voice Bible link

Do not invent a surname merely to fill the `fullName` field. Until a surname is
established in an approved episode, the character’s canonical first name is the full
name known to the series.

Episodes may add carefully reviewed facts and growth. They may not silently replace a
portrait, preference, relationship, habit, or voice. Update the Character Bible and
Season 1 Bible together whenever an episode establishes durable continuity.

Pup is always the Host. Pup is never the detective or narrator and never speaks for a
diner.
